    Eligibility, without the jargonEducation requirements and a limited NDA DOB checker

    NDA: education criteria

    • Army Wing: Class 12 pass or appearing.
    • Air Force, Naval Wing and 10+2 Naval Academy: Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or appearing.
    • Class 11 students are not eligible.

    Age, marital status, nationality and other conditions also apply.

    CDS & AFCAT: broad view

    • CDS: IMA/OTA generally require a recognised degree; INA requires an engineering degree; AFA has degree and subject conditions.
    • AFCAT: requirements differ across Flying, Technical and Non-Technical branches; some require specific degrees, subjects and minimum marks.

    Always check the academy- or branch-specific rule in the current notice.

    UPSC OTR / URN — explained simplyRegistration, common profile and exam application

    What are URN and CAF?

    URN (Universal Registration Number) is the common registration identity in UPSC's current online application system. CAF (Common Application Form) stores shared personal and educational profile details. You then complete the exam-specific application for the examination you want to take.

    1 · Create account
    Register on the official UPSC application portal.
    2 · Generate URN
    Complete universal registration and retain your login.
    3 · Complete CAF
    Enter profile details carefully.
    4 · Apply for exam
    Complete the exam-specific module, fee if applicable, and submit.
    Remember: A URN is common across exams; an application is specific to one exam. Creating a profile does not mean an exam application has been submitted.
    Common questionsNotices, registration and admit cards
    Is the annual calendar the official notification?

    No. The calendar gives a planned schedule. The individual notice provides final eligibility, DOB, vacancies, fee and instructions.

    Does creating a URN mean I have applied?

    No. Complete and submit the examination-specific application as well.

    Can I reuse NDA-II 2026's DOB range for NDA-I 2027?

    No. Each cycle has its own cut-off. Use only the NDA-I 2027 official notification for its DOB range.

    Where do I download my UPSC admit card?

    Use the official UPSC online application portal and follow the exam-specific instructions.

    Where do I apply for AFCAT?

    Use the official AFCAT portal. Vaani does not collect or store your login credentials.
